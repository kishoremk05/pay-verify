Why we are building this

"Disconnected departments. Slower government."

Each department hold a piece of the work and rarely share it. Systems do not connect. Processes are not written down or automated. So the work moves slowly, by phone call and email, one person at a time.

Finding what you need is the hard part of the job. The information exists, but the department that has it does not share a system with the department that needs it. So you call. You email. You wait. If you do not know how a process works, you ask whoever did it last. Work that should be automated is done by hand. None of this is the people's fault. The problem is that nothing connects, so every answer has to be chased.

Northgrid connects the work. One system across every department.

Everything you need to know.

Does Northgrid replace our system of record?

No. Northgrid sits alongside your existing systems and becomes authoritative for the workflows you choose, in the order you choose. Cutover is per-domain and reversible

How does this differ from a workflow tool or low-code platform?

Workflow tools express process. Northgrid expresses policy: typed, versioned, and reviewable by counsel. The audit log is not a feature; it is the substrate.

What about records retention and FOIA?

+ Retention schedules are applied at write, not after the fact. Open-records exports read directly from the audit log, with cryptographic verification of the chain.

Where is data hosted, and under what controls?

Single-tenant deployments in StateRAMP-authorized environments, with optional in-state residency. SOC 2 Type II controls apply across all tenants; jurisdiction-specific controls are negotiated in the brief.

Can our counsel review the policy engine?

Yes, and they should. Every rule renders to a plain-language preview alongside its typed definition. Counsel sign-off is a first-class step in our deployment process.

What happens if we want to leave?

A signed export of the full audit log, the rule sets in their typed form, and a documented schema is produced on request. No proprietary lock on the public record. Ever.
